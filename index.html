Replay

Left-click or tap to open a cell (on a flag it removes the flag and opens the cell). Right-click or long-press to flag. Click a satisfied number to chord. Keyboard (default keys): arrows move, D or Space opens, F flags, C chords, R restarts. C from anywhere else jumps back to the board (on your last cell, or the first one). L locks the page so nothing scrolls except the board; press L again to unlock. Every key can be changed in Settings, Keys tab. Drag the board to pan. Zoom with the buttons, Ctrl + wheel, pinch, or + and - on the keyboard; 1 resets the view. 2 resets the layout. Move the whole board by its grip bar, resize it from the bottom-right corner, and use Reset layout to put it back.

Scrollbars

Colors are kept per theme, like the Colors section. Hovering a scrollbar thumb lights it up in the accent color. Firefox only has a thin and a normal width, so there sizes up to 8 px are thin.

Cursors

Normal is the default pointer. Select shows while the left button is held. Flag shows on a right click, and over the board while flag mode is on. Holding shows while you drag the grip bar. Text and resize shows over text boxes and the resize corner. The page draws the cursor itself, so images of any size work, but it only follows a mouse (not touch). The hotspot is the point of the image that does the clicking.

Icons and images

Replace any icon with your own image (png, jpg, svg, webp, gif; up to 8 MB). Files stay in this browser only.

Board background

Pick an image or a video (video up to 64 MB). It sits under the board: hidden cells stay solid, cleared cells show it.

App background

Fills the whole page behind everything. Pick an image or a video (video up to 64 MB). The page background color shows when none is set. Video sound uses the Background video slider in the Audio tab.

Animations

Turn single effects on or off. Everything also stays off when your system's reduce-motion setting is on.

Key bindings

Click a slot, then press the key you want. Each action has two slots. Backspace clears a slot, Esc cancels. Chord and Jump to board can share a key: on the board it chords, anywhere else it jumps back to the board.
